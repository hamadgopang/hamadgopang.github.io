What is organic chemistry?

Organic chemistry is the study of carbon compounds. Carbon atoms form four covalent bonds and can bond with each other in chains, which is why there are so many organic compounds.

Big ideas
  • Hydrocarbons contain only carbon and hydrogen.
  • Alkanes, alkenes and alkynes differ in their carbon-carbon bonds.
  • A functional group decides how a compound behaves.
  • In a homologous series, successive members differ by CH2 and share the same functional group.
How the Lab works
  • School-level explanations first; advanced notes are labelled.
  • Every structure is drawn from curated, checked data.
  • Reaction animations show the overall change, not the detailed mechanism.
  • Use Teacher Mode for full-screen classroom demonstrations.

Teacher Mode

Every tool has a Teacher Mode button. It opens a full-screen classroom view with large formulas and structures, high contrast and keyboard control, so the site can be used directly on a smart board or projector.

Arrow keys step through animations and topics, Space plays or flips, F toggles browser fullscreen and Esc leaves.
