      Bromine-water and combustion notes are qualitative classroom tests. Real experiments need teacher supervision.

      Bond comparison

      The same two carbons can share one, two or three pairs of electrons. Shared pairs: single 1, double 2, triple 3.

      Bond formation (overall change)

      Removing two hydrogen atoms from ethane and sharing a second electron pair gives ethene (C=C). This animation shows the overall change, not the detailed mechanism.

      Compare families

      For the same number of carbons, unsaturated compounds hold fewer hydrogen atoms.

      Assumptions: acyclic chains with at most one multiple bond. General formulas are CnH2n+2 (alkanes), CnH2n (alkenes) and CnH2n−2 (alkynes).
