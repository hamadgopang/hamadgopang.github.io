# Hamad Khan

**Chemistry Educator · Alipur, Muzaffargarh, Pakistan**

This website is the professional portfolio of Hamad Khan, a Chemistry educator with fifteen years of teaching experience. It presents his work with Matriculation students (Classes 9 and 10), his approach to clear and practical science teaching, and the learning resources he has developed for the classroom.

## About Hamad

Hamad specialises in making Chemistry understandable, practical and inspiring. He teaches core topics through structured whiteboard lessons, smart-board presentations, laboratory demonstrations and carefully chosen digital tools. His focus is on strong concepts, examination readiness and helping students see science as something they can understand and enjoy.

He has taught across a number of schools in Alipur and nearby areas, and prepares his own notes, lectures and examination material in both English and Urdu. Alongside teaching, he also has experience in agriculture, including crop work, soil identification and testing, and modern farming practices.

## What you will find on this site

- An introduction to Hamad’s teaching philosophy, experience and skills
- An overview of classroom methods, from traditional teaching to simulations and laboratory work
- Educational resources and study materials prepared for Matriculation students
- An interactive **Periodic Table Explorer** for learning the elements in English and Urdu
- An **Organic Chemistry Lab** for exploring hydrocarbons, functional groups, reactions, quizzes and flashcards, with a Teacher Mode suitable for classroom display
- Contact details for educational collaboration or academic discussion
