About

About the Educator

Passionate about making science easier to understand and more interesting to explore.

I am Hamad Khan, a passionate and dedicated Chemistry Educator with 15 years of teaching experience, specializing in Chemistry for Matriculation students (Classes 9th and 10th). My teaching philosophy is centered on making complex scientific concepts simple, understandable, engaging, and practically relevant for students.

Throughout my teaching career, I have worked with several educational institutions in Alipur and surrounding areas, helping students develop a strong understanding of chemistry through effective teaching methodologies, conceptual explanations, and practical demonstrations.

I believe that chemistry is best understood when theoretical knowledge is connected with real-world applications. Therefore, I incorporate modern teaching technologies into my classrooms, including interactive smart boards, digital presentations, PhET simulations, Sketchfab 3D visualizations, educational animations, and laboratory experiments.

In addition to traditional whiteboard teaching, I develop my own PowerPoint lectures, educational presentations, examination papers, and comprehensive study notes in both English and Urdu. My self-prepared chemistry notes have also been made available through local bookshops in Alipur, serving as additional learning material for students.

Alongside my teaching career, I have two years of experience in agriculture, including wheat, rice and maize (corn) crops, soil identification and testing, and the use of advanced agricultural techniques. This has further strengthened my understanding of practical applications of science in everyday life.

My ultimate goal is to make chemistry an interesting, accessible, and enjoyable subject while helping students build strong concepts, develop scientific thinking, and achieve academic excellence.
